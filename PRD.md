### 2.2 Local Market Pain Points & ELFY Counter-Measures
1. **Shoe Sizing Anxiety (Keraguan Saiz Kaki)**:
   - *Problem*: Malaysian foot morphology often features a wider forefoot (Asian wide fit) compared to narrow European lasts. Sizing discrepancies between EU, UK, and US cause hesitations.
   - *Solution*:
     - Shoes crafted with an accommodating **E-width (Comfort Wide Toe Box)**.
     - Interactive **Bottom-Sheet Size Recommender (CM to EU/UK)**.
     - Bold Reassurance: **"Jaminan Tukar Saiz 7 Hari Percuma"** (Free 1-to-1 size exchange picked up right from the customer's doorstep).
2. **Tropical Climate & Wearability (Cuaca Lembap & Panas)**:
   - *Problem*: Heavy shoes cause foot perspiration and odor; cheap watches fog up in high humidity or sudden heavy tropical downpours.
   - *Solution*:
     - Footwear equipped with **Ortholite®-grade antibacterial memory foam insoles** and breathable pigskin/microfiber linings.
     - Slip-on loafers designed with easy-entry heel collars (**Wudhu / Solat friendly** for quick removal at prayer rooms/surau during workdays).
     - Watches guaranteed with **5ATM to 10ATM water resistance** with double O-ring crown gaskets and sapphire-coated scratch-resistant crystals.
3. **Payment Security & Trust Hesitation**:
   - *Problem*: High drop-offs when forced to enter credit card details on unknown sites.
   - *Solution*: Prominent Malaysian checkout rails: **FPX** (Maybank2u, CIMB Clicks, Public Bank), **Touch 'n Go eWallet**, **GrabPay**, and **Buy Now Pay Later (Atome / SPayLater)** for 3x interest-free installments of ~RM 80/month.

## 6. Marketing Signals & Signal Engine Architecture

1. **Deterministic `event_id` Deduplication**:
   - `PageView`: Generated on SSR loader and hydrated to client.
   - `ViewContent`: Emitted on PDP view with exact SKU, Ringgit amount, and category tag.
   - `AddToCart`: Emitted when variant pill is added to bag with matching `event_id` sent to both `fbq` and server CAPI.
   - `InitiateCheckout`: Fired on drawer checkout CTA click.
2. **Cross-Domain Cart Attributes Bridge**:
   - Captures `_fbp`, `_fbc`, `_ga`, `gclid`, and `fbclid` from URL/cookies.
   - Injects into cart attributes:
     - `_attribution_fbp`
     - `_attribution_fbc`
     - `_attribution_gclid`
     - `_attribution_ga`
   - Preserves complete attribution through Shopify's hosted checkout to downstream `orders/paid` webhooks.
3. **Google Ads Enhanced Conversions**:
   - Injects GTM container with CSP nonces.
   - Prepares customer data hashing (SHA-256 phone/email) for offline/webhook conversion signals.
